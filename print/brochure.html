Manual inspection drifts

People get tired, standards vary between shifts, and nothing records why a part was passed.

Vision projects are fragile

Every change needs the original integrator, and a fault can quietly pass parts that should have been rejected.

Machine data stays in the PLC

Counts, stops and alarms stay on the machine, so OEE and quality reports are built by hand.

Fail-closed by design

Bad parts don’t slip through when something breaks

A part is OK only when every camera view reports OK. Errors, timeouts and missing views all mean NG (rejected), and the result is written down before the machine hears it.

  1. TriggerThe PLC signals a part. Line safety is checked on every trigger.
  2. Capture every viewEach camera runs the checks in the signed program.
  3. A view faultsA camera times out. A missing result counts as a failed one.
  4. Write the recordVerdict, image and reason are saved first.
  5. NG to the PLCOnly then does the machine get the result.
